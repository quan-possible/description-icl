## Grids in powers of ten

Bruce stopped the network training I had relaunched: compute the exact
results first and look before training. The exact runs (locally, in tmux)
confirmed the tenfold grids. Bruce's rule: choose the ends that matter, a
factor of 2 or 10 apart, instead of many nearby values; examples in hand
are reported at $n = 0, 1, 10, 100$, which land on the four regimes of
Proposition 3 (alone, identified, below the dimension, verified: 23, 30,
29, 88 examples at $d = 16$, $r = 0.001$, $p = 0.9$). Decisions 21 to 23 in
`docs/wiki/experiments.md`.

Consequences: Table 1 keeps $d = 4$ as an illustration of the error term
$1/(rd)$ rather than of the approximation; the networks take $r \in \{0.1,
0.01\}$ because a description worth 100 examples has no crossing inside a
20-example prompt at $d = 5$; the single-query to long-horizon ratio tops
out at 3.3 on this grid (8.5 came from $r = 0.9$).
