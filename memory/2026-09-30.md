## Two derivations written out

Proposition 1 now states the high-SNR ESS of a reliable description as
$(d-1)(1-r) + O(1/(rd))$, from $\psi(\tfrac{d-n}{2}) - \psi(\tfrac d2) =
\log r$; at `a0` $= 100$ the exact values are within 0.1. Proposition 2 is
the two-sided bound $p R_{p=1}(r) + (1-p) R^{\mathrm{ex}}(0) \le R \le
\text{same} + H(p)$ for every $r$, proved by a KL argument and the
mixture-density inequality. Every number in the paper's tables was checked
against the committed CSVs.

## Networks trained on Colab

Bruce approved Colab, chose log loss (decision 19), one seed per setting
(decision 20), and accepted a fixed 40k-step budget confirmed by the pilot
rather than a chosen stopping point. Four L4 VMs ran in parallel: pilot plus
coarse reliable on one, the two $p = 0.9$ models on their own, then a
continuation of the precise unreliable model. bf16 autocast doubled
throughput to 10 steps/s; the L4 is saturated by one process.

Results: reliable descriptions reproduced (ESS 6.7 vs 6.7, 2.2 vs 2.3);
coarse unreliable reproduced (1.8 vs 1.9); precise unreliable off (3.9 vs
5.0, implied trust 0.85, loss still falling at 40k). Transfer: the model
trained on precise reliable descriptions gets 0.7 examples' worth on
$p = 0.9$ prompts against a calibrated 5.0, like a fully trusting Bayes
learner. Section 6 of the paper is drafted from the generated table and
figure.

`sessions.json`; and the design record must not be named `DESIGN.md`.
